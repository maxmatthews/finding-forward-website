This is the first entry in what I'm calling **Practice Notes** — a small,
unhurried place for the kind of thinking I do between sessions and wish I had
more time to share with the clients I work with.

It will probably look like a few things:

- Short essays on ideas from DBT and self-compassion, written for the people
  actually using them, not for clinicians.
- Reflections on patterns I keep seeing — the ones that don't show up in any
  textbook but show up in a lot of weeks.
- Occasional book notes, almost certainly some Pirsig, possibly Linehan.

I'll write here when I have something honest to say. No schedule.

If something here speaks to you — or, more usefully, *bothers* you in a way
that's hard to name — that's often worth following. I'd be glad to talk.

— Kevin
